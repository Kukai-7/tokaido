# 東海道 · The Tōkaidō Journey

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://kukai-7.github.io/tokaido/)**

A cinematic walk along the Tōkaidō, the great road of the Edo period, from Nihonbashi in Edo (Tokyo) all the way to merchant Naniwa (Osaka), told through the prints of **Utagawa Hiroshige**'s *Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō* (1833–1834).

- An interactive map of the road and its relay stations, traced on Japan's real coastline
- Hiroshige's print for each station along the way
- A seal (印) to collect at each station, saved in your browser so you can come back to it later
- Works on desktop and mobile

*The experience is written in French.*

## Built with

A single, self-contained HTML file: vanilla JavaScript, SVG, no framework. Prints are public-domain works loaded from Wikimedia Commons.
